Release controls reduce modeled exposure, but the closing decision remains unresolved

Owner: Risk & Ops Strategy · Status: Meeting draft · Evidence cutoff: 2026-09-18

Decision

Risk Committee to approve mandatory release and closing gates for the first launch by 2026-09-30, with the sixteen uncovered scenarios assigned to named owners before go-live.

Motivation

Today, control coverage is mapped but not measured in production. That gap matters because a mapped control can look complete while providing no verified protection. This analysis tests which controls would surface a stale approval before release.

Evidence

  • 29 event frequencies measured from historical records; the remaining 176 are derived from measured anchors.
  • Modeled unresolved-release exposure falls from 84% to 10% in the tested scenario, across the 500-deal simulation; the result is directional and depends on assumed control effectiveness.
  • 189 of 205 risk scenarios have a mapped control; production effectiveness has not yet been measured.

Material uncertainty

The recommendation assumes controls operate at 84% of the tested rate. If production operation falls below roughly 60%, modeled exposure no longer clears the risk-tolerance threshold. Instrument bypasses and overrides after launch to test this assumption within 90 days.
